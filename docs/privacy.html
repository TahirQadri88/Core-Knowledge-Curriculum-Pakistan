Privacy and terms

Ilm Track: Core Knowledge Tracker for Pakistani Students. Last updated 12 October 2026.

In short. Ilm Track keeps your children’s progress on your phone. If you sign in to share it with your family, a copy is kept on our server so your family’s phones can see it. We do not show ads, sell data or track you across other sites.

If you do not sign in

Everything stays on your phone, in your browser’s storage. Nothing about your children is sent to us. Clearing the browser’s site data removes it, so save a backup file now and then (More, then Backup).

If you sign in for family sync

A parent signs in with Google. From Google we receive only your first name, your email address and an account number that identifies you. We never see your Google password.

To share progress between your family’s phones, our server keeps:

  • Your name and email, and the family name you choose.
  • Who is in the family: parents, and any child’s phone added with a code (named like “Aisha’s phone”). A child’s phone needs no email or Google account.
  • Each child’s details as you enter them: name, class, age if given, school track, books and chapters, routine items and Qur’an reading.
  • Each child’s progress: ticks, notes, character levels, surahs memorised and revised, and routine ticks, with their dates.
  • A sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in for up to 60 days. We store only a scrambled form of it.
  • Family codes, also scrambled, which work once and expire after 7 days.
  • To stop people guessing codes: the network address of wrong code attempts and how many there were, deleted after 24 hours.

Only members of your family can see your family’s data. The app is built so that each request checks that you belong to the family.

Who else is involved

  • Cloudflare hosts the app and its database (Cloudflare Pages and D1).
  • Google provides sign-in. Google’s sign-in script loads only on the Family screen when you are signed out.
  • Google Fonts provides the app’s fonts.

Each of these handles network requests under its own privacy policy. We do not use analytics or advertising services.

Removing your data

  • Sign out or Leave this family (More, then Family): this phone stops syncing and keeps its own copy.
  • Delete family from the server (the parent who started the family): removes the family, its members and all its children’s progress from our server. Each phone keeps its own copy until you remove it.
  • To remove your sign-in record (name and email) as well, email tahir.razavi@gmail.com from the same address.

Children

Ilm Track is for parents. Parents enter and manage their children’s progress. A child’s phone can join only with a code from a parent, and can change only ticks, notes, Qur’an and routine.

Terms of use

  • Ilm Track is free, offered as it is, while it is still being built and tested. Features may change.
  • Keep your own backup. We work to keep your data safe but cannot promise it will never be lost.
  • Class goals are plain-language summaries of Pakistan’s National Curriculum. They are a guide for parents, not an official document.
  • Ilm Track is independent. It is not affiliated with the National Curriculum Council, the Core Knowledge Foundation or Oxford University Press. Core Knowledge materials come from coreknowledge.org under the Foundation’s terms.
  • Use the app for your own family. Do not try to reach other families’ data.
