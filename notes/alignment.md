# Pakistan's National Curriculum compared with the Core Knowledge Sequence

Compared on 12 October 2026, class by class, KG to Class 8. Pakistani Class N is set against U.S. Grade N (KG against Kindergarten).

- Pakistan: the National Curriculum Council's progression grids on ncc.gov.pk (NCP 2020 for KG to Class 5, 2022-23 for Classes 6 to 8), the same sources as the Class goals tab. Codes are the grids' SLO numbers.
- International standard: the Core Knowledge Sequence, 2023 edition, https://www.coreknowledge.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/CK_Sequence2023_GK8_W3.pdf (Core Knowledge maths and English follow the U.S. Common Core; science follows the Next Generation Science Standards). "CK Seq p. N" is the PDF page.
- This compares curriculum documents, not textbooks. A school's books may cover some gaps listed here.
- U.S.-only history and U.S. units of measure are not counted as gaps. Topics the owner chose to leave out of the app (evolution, puberty and reproduction, Israel and Palestine, and religious texts of other faiths) are recorded below for completeness but never shown to parents.
- The app shows up to five extras per subject per class (`INTL` in `src/data.js`), chosen from the gaps below: first topics Pakistan does not teach by Class 8, then topics Pakistan teaches two or more years later.

Columns: **Gaps** = Core Knowledge teaches it and Pakistan does not by Class 8. **Earlier in CK** = Pakistan teaches it later (class in brackets). **Earlier in Pakistan** = Pakistan teaches it first.


## Mathematics

Pakistan's National Curriculum maths and the Core Knowledge Sequence (built on the U.S. Common Core) cover much the same ground from KG to Class 8, and the grade levels mostly match. Pakistan is often a year or more ahead in arithmetic: times tables, fractions, decimals, percent, HCF and LCM, angle facts, and algebraic identities. Core Knowledge is ahead on the coordinate grid (Grade 5), volume (Grade 5), inequalities (Grade 6) and data: line plots, dot plots, box plots and measures of spread. The main gaps for a Karachi child are statistics and sampling, functions, scientific notation, scatter plots, and slicing solids, which Pakistan leaves to Class 9 or later or does not teach. Pakistan alone teaches Roman numbers, the Islamic calendar, sets and Venn diagrams, compass constructions, and business maths such as zakat, ushr, tax and inheritance.

## Science (with General Knowledge for Classes 1-3 and Early Childhood for KG)

Pakistan's science (with General Knowledge in Classes 1-3 and ECE in KG) covers most of the same ground as Core Knowledge Science, but often one to four years later, especially in the early classes. Core Knowledge starts forces, magnets, light, sound, the solar system, states of matter, cells and body systems in Kindergarten to Grade 2, which Pakistan leaves to Classes 3-6. Topics the Pakistan grids do not teach at all by Class 8 include fossils, how the eye and ear work, the muscular and excretory systems, nuclear power, Earth's spheres, plate tectonics, the electromagnetic spectrum, ocean currents, hormones and puberty, Mendel's genetics, mutations and evolution. Pakistan in turn teaches more chemistry (Periodic Table, solutions, acids and pH), electric circuits, immunity and diseases, and Pakistan-specific health and biotechnology. Pakistan's earth science sits partly in Social Studies and Geography, so those grids were checked before calling a topic missing.

## English

Pakistan's English curriculum and CK Language Arts teach the same core skills, and from Class 4 Pakistan's grid copies many U.S. Common Core outcomes almost word for word. The main skills gap is timing: CK finishes phonics and teaches quotation marks, letters, compound and complex sentences, idioms and Greek and Latin roots one to four years earlier, and it sets reading-speed targets. CK also teaches research projects with notes, citations and a bibliography, plus active and passive voice and conditional mood; Pakistan does not ask for these by Class 8. The biggest gap is knowledge: Pakistan names no books, poems, myths or speeches, while CK sets a reading list each year (fables and fairy tales, Greek and Norse myths, Arabian Nights, Treasure Island, Shakespeare, Homer, classic poems and sayings). Pakistan goes further on functional English (formal letters, emails, direct and indirect speech, noun gender), so a Karachi child mainly needs CK's literature, sayings, research and writing about literature added.

## History and Geography (with General Knowledge, Social Studies and Early Childhood)

Core Knowledge teaches world history and geography years earlier than Pakistan: maps, continents and oceans from KG and Grade 1, Mesopotamia, Egypt, India, China, Greece and Rome by Grade 3, medieval Europe and early Islam by Grade 4, where Pakistan reaches most of these in Classes 4 to 7. The biggest gaps for a Pakistani child are regions Pakistan never studies: Africa's kingdoms, imperial China and the Mongols, Japan and Korea, the Americas and Latin America. Pakistan also leaves out the Age of Exploration, the Reformation, the French and Industrial Revolutions, capitalism and socialism, the Russian Revolution and the Cold War. Pakistan is stronger on its own story (the Indus Valley, Gandhara, the Delhi Sultanate and Mughals, the Pakistan Movement and Pakistan since 1947) and on civics and everyday economics from Class 3. Much of Core Knowledge's Grade 1 to 7 American strand is U.S.-only and is not a gap.

- **Gaps:** Russian Revolution, Lenin and the Soviet Union (CK Seq p. 236, Grade 7 XII.C); Rise of fascism and Nazism: Mussolini, Hitler, Stalin (CK Seq p. 237, Grade 7 XIV.A); Cold War: Iron Curtain, NATO, Berlin Wall, arms race (CK Seq p. 239, Grade 7 XV.A, C); Korean War and Vietnam War (CK Seq p. 239, Grade 7 XV.B, D); End of Cold War, end of apartheid, Gulf War (CK Seq p. 241, Grade 7 XVI.C)
- **Earlier in CK:** First World War: alliances, trenches, Treaty of Versailles: CK grade 7, Pakistan class 8 (CK Seq p. 236, Grade 7 XII.B, History Grade 8 topic 3); Second World War and the Holocaust: CK grade 7, Pakistan class 8 (CK Seq p. 237-238, Grade 7 XIV, History Grade 8 topic 3)
- **Only in Pakistan's curriculum:** Muslims in South Asia: Delhi Sultanate and Mughal Empire (History Grade 7 topic 4); Seljuk, Fatimid, Safavid and Ottoman dynasties (History Grade 7 topic 3); Umayyad and Abbasid caliphates by name (History Grade 7 topic 2); Farming, land use, population; urban and rural life (G-07-B-01 to B-12); Pollution and changing landforms (G-07-C-01, C-02)

### Class 8

Both cover British India, the World Wars and 1947 in Class 8; Core Knowledge's world survey adds China, Africa, the Cold War and the modern Middle East.

- **Gaps:** Imperialism: scramble for Africa, Suez Canal, French Indochina (CK Seq p. 294-298, Grade 8 XVIII.B, D, E, J); Unification of Italy and of Germany (CK Seq p. 296-297, Grade 8 XVIII.F-G); Opening of Japan, Opium Wars, end of imperial China (CK Seq p. 297, Grade 8 XVIII.H-I); Communist China: Mao, Long March, Cultural Revolution (CK Seq p. 303-304, Grade 8 XX.B-D); Korea, Japan and Southeast Asia, ancient to modern (CK Seq p. 279-280, 303-306, Grade 8 XI, XX); Cold War Europe, Soviet collapse, Yugoslavia, European Union (CK Seq p. 306-308, Grade 8 XXI); End of empire in Africa; apartheid and South Africa (CK Seq p. 309-311, Grade 8 XXII.C-F); Israel, Palestine and the Arab-Israeli wars (CK Seq p. 311-312, Grade 8 XXII.H); Latin America in the 20th century: Cuba, Brazil, Argentina (CK Seq p. 313-317, Grade 8 XXIII); Arab Spring, Syrian war, refugees and migration (CK Seq p. 318-319, Grade 8 XXIV.D-E)
- **Only in Pakistan's curriculum:** Reform movements in British India (History Grade 8 topic 2); Political awakening and the road to Pakistan (History Grade 8 topic 4); Pakistan, a new country: 1947 to today (History Grade 8 topic 5); CPEC and infrastructure (G-08-B-06); Fisheries, minerals and energy resources (G-08-B-01); GPS and GIS mapping tools (G-08-A-03, B-07); Earthquakes, rivers, rock cycle (CK puts this in Science) (G-08-A-01)
