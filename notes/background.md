# Background

## Where this came from
1. A Gemini report on the Core Knowledge curriculum for Karachi (Sindh Board and O Level families). Its citations were unusable: the same few pages were repeated under paragraphs they did not support. Errors found: O Level Literature in English is syllabus 2010, not "2027"; the claimed Literature set texts were not confirmed; not all CKLA editions are free; it missed that CKHG Grade 4 already has a unit on Medieval Islamic Empires.
2. A corrected, cited daily plan was written as a Claude Doc ("Core Knowledge × Islamic Tarbiyah: A Daily Plan for Karachi Families"): a homeschool day and a 75-minute after-school routine built around the five prayers, a worked Grade 4 week, and how Core Knowledge lines up with Cambridge O Level English 1123 (exams 2027–2028).
3. This app turns that plan into a tracker, then adds Oxford school books and the character list from Tahir's article.

## Key facts used in the plan (all checked 11 Oct 2026)
- Core Knowledge Foundation: founded 1986 by E. D. Hirsch Jr., Charlottesville. Sequence 2023 edition.
- Simple View of Reading: Gough & Tunmer (1986), Remedial and Special Education 7, 6–10.
- Cambridge O Level English Language 1123 (2027–2028): Paper 1 Reading 2 h, 50 marks (Text A about 900 words; summary of at most 150 words from a 550–600 word Text B); Paper 2 Writing 2 h, 50 marks (directed writing 250–350 words from 400–450 words of text; composition 350–450 words).
- Sindh Textbook Board free e-books: http://ebooks.stbb.edu.pk/

## Earlier versions
- Version 1 of the app linked units to Online Resources PDFs (lists of extra links). Families could not find the actual units. Version 2 links each unit to its download page with the Teacher Guide, Reader and Activity Book.
